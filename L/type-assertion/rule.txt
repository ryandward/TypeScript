type-assertion
kind: soundness
TS2896 type assertion: checkAssertion

A type assertion claims a value has a type without checking it, and the checker takes its word. The lab
rejects every as T and <T>x except as const, which claims nothing the literal doesn't show.

Where it's enforced, in src/compiler/checker.ts:
- checkAssertion reports TS2896 for every assertion whose type isn't const.

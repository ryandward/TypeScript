kind: soundness
TS2897 definite assignment assertion: checkPropertyDeclaration

A definite assignment assertion claims a property is assigned without checking it, and the checker takes
its word. The lab rejects every property declared with !. A let or var declared with ! has no initializer,
so single assignment already rejects it (TS2892).

Where it's enforced, in src/compiler/checker.ts:
- checkPropertyDeclaration reports TS2897 for a property declaration with !.

Library declarations that collide with the rule: TypeORM entity columns, which TypeORM assigns.

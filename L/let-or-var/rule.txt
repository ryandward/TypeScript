let-or-var
kind: policy

Claim. If the lab accepts a program, every binding holds the value it was declared with, from its declaration
on.

Counterexample in plain TypeScript. A let or var may be declared without a value and assigned later, and
TypeScript assumes a variable read from inside another function is initialized if it is assigned anywhere, so
a hoisted function can read it while it is still undefined. TypeScript's types are honest about reassignment
itself; L forbids it for its own goals, and the witness shows what it costs.

Obligation 1, binding without an initializer: TS2892
  Statement. Every binding the program creates gets its value where it is declared.
  Mechanism. checkGrammarVariableDeclaration reports TS2892 for a let or var declared without an initializer.
  Loop, catch and ambient bindings aren't held to it: the loop or the throw binds them, and an ambient
  declaration describes a binding made elsewhere.
  Why it suffices. A declaration is the only place a binding is created, and every declaration either carries
  a value or is one of those three.
  Witnesses:
    chk-captured-let.ts                the declaration without a value
  Controls:
    ambient-declaration.ts             declare var describes a global that already exists
    catch-binding.ts                   a catch variable, bound by the throw
    for-of-binding.ts                  a for-of variable, bound by the loop

Obligation 2, assignment to a binding: TS2893
  Statement. No binding is assigned after its declaration.
  Mechanism. checkIdentifier reports TS2893 for any assignment to a variable after its declaration,
  parameters included.
  Why it suffices. Every assignment to a binding, plain, compound, increment or destructuring, resolves its
  target through checkIdentifier, which sees that it is being assigned.
  Witnesses:
    chk-captured-let.ts                the assignment after the declaration
  Controls:
    let-never-reassigned.ts            a let with an initializer, never assigned again
  Rejected sound code, because L forbids reassignment even where it is safe:
    counting-loop.ts                   a counting loop reassigns its counter on every step

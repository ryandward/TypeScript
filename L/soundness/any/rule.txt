any

A type written any accepts every value and allows every use of it, so the checker checks nothing about the
value. The lab rejects the keyword wherever it's written: in a type annotation, a type argument, a constraint,
or a declaration file. Keeping a library's declarations out of the check is the project's choice, made with
skipLibCheck, a declare module override or an adapter.

Where it's enforced, in src/compiler/checker.ts:
- checkAnyKeyword reports TS2898 for every any keyword.

type-guard-or-assertion-function
kind: soundness
TS2899 type predicate on a function: checkTypePredicate

A predicate written on a function, x is T or asserts x is T, claims what the function's body proves, and the
checker takes its word without reading the body. The lab rejects every predicate written on a function,
including an overload's, whose body is the implementation's. With no predicate written, the checker infers one
from the body and checks it. A predicate in a function type, an interface or an abstract method is a
requirement: assignment checks that the function put there proves it.

Where it's enforced, in src/compiler/checker.ts:
- checkTypePredicate reports TS2899 for a predicate on a function, method, arrow function or function
  expression that isn't abstract.

non-null-assertion
kind: soundness

Claim. If the lab accepts a program, no value is treated as present unless a check the checker read showed it
is.

Counterexample in plain TypeScript. x! removes null and undefined from x's type without a check:
checkNonNullAssertion takes the assertion's word.

Obligation 1, non-null assertion: TS2895
  Statement. A value's type may lose null and undefined only through a check the checker reads, such as a
  comparison or a typeof test.
  Mechanism. checkNonNullAssertion reports TS2895 for every x! expression.
  Why it suffices. x! is the only expression that removes null and undefined without a check; as and the
  definite assignment ! are their own rules, type-assertion and definite-assignment.
  Witnesses:
    rule-nonnull.ts                    an element the array doesn't have, asserted present
  Controls:
    checked-presence.ts                presence checked, and the checker narrows after the check
    logical-not.ts                     a prefix ! is a logical not
  Rejected sound code, because Map.get's type can't say the key is there:
    map-get-after-set.ts               the key was just set

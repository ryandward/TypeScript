let-or-var

Every binding is assigned once, where it is declared. The keywords themselves are allowed, so a let that's
initialized and never reassigned compiles.

Where it's enforced, in src/compiler/checker.ts:
- checkGrammarVariableDeclaration reports TS2892 for a let or var declared without an initializer. Loop,
  catch and ambient bindings aren't held to it, since the program doesn't assign them.
- checkIdentifier reports TS2893 for any assignment to a variable after its declaration, including
  parameters.
